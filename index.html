Phodijos Childcare Lakeside Niños

At Phodijos Childcare Lakeside Niños, we believe every child deserves a safe, joyful and inclusive space to learn and grow. Our culture celebrates diversity, kindness and respect where every child’s background, language and personality are valued. We nurture curiosity, creativity and confidence through play, learning and love.

Our Story

As a mum with a deep passion for children, I created Phodijos Childcare so that all my children and every child who joins us can understand how wonderful it is to care for others while learning to care for themselves. My journey began when, at a very young age, I looked after two children who were abandoned on my mother's door step. I was mostly responsible in caring for them due to being the oldest female. That experience sparked a lifelong love for childcare and shaped the way I see nurturing as both a privilege and a purpose.

When I later had my own children, that passion only grew stronger. I realised how fulfilling it is to help children feel safe, loved and understood and I wanted to share that feeling with families in my community.

The Meaning Behind Our Name

Phodijos is more than a name — it’s a symbol of family and inclusivity.

  • Pho stands for my first daughter.
  • Di represents my second daughter.
  • Jos honours my youngest son.

Together, their names remind me that every child, no matter who they are, deserves care, love and belonging.

Our Promise

We treat every child as if they were our own with compassion and joy. Through Phodijos Childcare, I continue to live out my calling, creating a space where children grow to feel valued and learn that kindness starts with how we care for one another.

02 / Safeguarding

Keeping Children Safe in Our Childcare Setting

Ofsted-registered childminder based in a safe, nurturing home environment. I am DBS-checked, Paediatric First Aid certified, and trained as a Designated Safeguarding Lead (DSL), giving you peace of mind that your child is in safe and caring hands.

Safeguarding is not a policy on a shelf. It is the way we welcome, listen, supervise, record and respond, every single day.

🛡

In our childminding environment

In our childminding environment, safeguarding is built into everything we do. We follow consistent routines, safety checks and emergency procedures to make sure every child is protected.

Regular Evacuation Procedures

We carry out a monthly evacuation drill to ensure everyone both staff and children knows exactly what to do in an emergency as these practices help children become familiar with the routine, reduce panic and ensure staff respond confidently and efficiently.

⌂

Secure Entry and Collection Procedures

To protect children from unauthorised access:

  • Doors remain locked during operating hours
  • Only approved adults can collect children
  • Visitors are supervised at all times
  • Identity verification is required if someone new is collecting

This ensures children are always handed over safely.

♡

Daily Safety Checks

Before children arrive, we complete thorough checks of the environment, including:

  • Ensuring all indoor and outdoor areas are hazard-free
  • Checking safety gates, locks and alarms
  • Making sure toys and equipment are clean, safe and age-appropriate
  • Removing or securing anything that could pose a risk
